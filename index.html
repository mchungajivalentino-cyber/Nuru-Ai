Our Story

A Voice Forged in Faith,
Offered in Love

✝ Diocese of Central Tanganyika (DCT)

Mchungaji Valentino — whose name means 'shepherd' — is a priest of the Anglican Church, Diocese of Central Tanganyika (DCT), Dodoma, Tanzania. Under the spiritual leadership of Bp Rev. Dr. Dickson Chilongani PhD, he has served faithfully for over seven years, walking alongside communities that the powerful often overlook and the comfortable easily forget.

"This is not a platform. It is a presence. A reminder that every soul deserves a shepherd who will not abandon them when the terrain grows rough."

NURU AI — meaning 'light' in Swahili — bridges the ancient wisdom of scripture with the honest needs of modern life, offering AI-powered spiritual counsel, pastoral teachings, and a community of faith to all who seek.
